> MOC: [20_omp_chase_MOC](./20_omp_chase_MOC.md) · G2 · ⬜ · P2 — Keep plan models, titles, compaction, rewind, and disposal bound to the live session generation.

## Chase Scope

This card covers session disposal, plan-mode exit and model restoration, deferred role switches, plan-review dispatch, title generation, provider-session reuse, checkpoint/rewind branching, and plan-reference compaction under an active goal. These commits share one lifecycle rule: asynchronous work may complete only against the session, mode, and generation that started it.

JWC already has inline plan-model transitions, issue-816 coverage, provider-session state, checkpoint/rewind tools, active-goal continuation, compaction, and an async disposal transaction. It does not have OMP's extracted `plan-mode/model-transition.ts` owner or its title-disposal regression. Adaptation should strengthen the existing owners rather than introduce a parallel plan lifecycle.

All 15 matching commits are retained. Warp stop/session-stop signaling and registry park/dispose changes remain with their dedicated cards.

- [ ] Prove failed plan entry and exit restore the previous model, thinking level, tools, and mode atomically.
- [ ] Cancel or generation-gate title work during dispose and session replacement.
- [ ] Preserve provider-session continuity only when title generation uses the same live session/model identity.
- [ ] Verify checkpoint/rewind branches retain the report and continuation classification without replaying discarded turns.
- [ ] Run plan-reference compaction coverage while a JWC goal is active.
- [ ] Keep plan-review overlay changes subordinate to completed title persistence and execution dispatch.
