## Why This Was A Gap

GJC expanded Telegram notifications from a basic completion/control channel into a richer remote operations surface. The important slices are global rich Telegram rendering, opt-in live turn streaming with in-place Telegram edits, slash-command control frames, stronger ask-reply acknowledgement, persisted update dedupe, redaction/file-send hardening, and notification noise suppression.

JWC has a notification foundation, but its files are not a direct copy of the new GJC layout. Current JWC owners use split modules such as `daemon-engine.ts`, `daemon-runtime.ts`, `telegram-inbound-router.ts`, `threaded-surface.ts`, `notification-redaction.ts`, and `telegram-media-policy.ts`; the upstream commits still target `telegram-daemon.ts`, `threaded-render.ts`, `rich-render.ts`, and `rich-draft.ts`. This card is therefore an adaptation card, not a blind import card.

## Interview Decision (2026-07-09)
