> Reviewed source: OMP `7aa1d581c..b0d04e517` (`origin/main`, v16.4.2→v17.0.1) vs JWC worktree `44c9fa8fa+dirty`.

## Chase Scope

OMP replaces a single model selector with a hub/browser/picker system. It adds two-pane keyboard focus, dynamic hub ordering, custom-role management, quality-ranked search, temporary selection, and role-aware thinking preservation. The final commit also applies tiered fuzzy search to sessions.

JWC already treats model choice as a role contract rather than a flat provider list. The useful reference is therefore the behavior boundary: `default`, `executor_ext`, `architect`, `planner`, `critic`, and discovered custom roles must remain coherent; temporary picks must not rewrite persistent role settings; and thinking levels must follow the selected role. OMP's component split is not itself an adoption requirement.
