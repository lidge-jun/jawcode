## WP6 Resolution — 2026-07-11

**ACCEPT against the completed JWC stack.** No duplicate reply-sent store or daemon route table was
introduced. JWC makes the session registry authoritative, revalidates every forwarded answer, records
idempotency before returning acceptance, and isolates callback acknowledgement failures. Reply routes
derive from the current discovery pendingAction and disappear when that snapshot clears. The ask bridge
clears its single-shot remote listener in finally, and session startup suppresses every non-root task depth.

The daemon classifier records update ids only after paired-chat/topic authorization and exposes dedupe
injection to the daemon owner. Adding GJC's monolithic daemon-path store would duplicate transport-state
ownership and was rejected. Ask/registry/router/inbound tests are included in the 239-pass WP6 run.

## Scope

Import the reliability and security hardening around Telegram ask replies, acknowledgement ordering, update dedupe persistence, reply-route cleanup, unattended gate listener disposal, and subagent notification suppression. This slice should preserve JWC's split notification owners instead of copying GJC's monolithic `telegram-daemon.ts` layout.
