## Chase Scope

This card tracks race safety for self-continuing long workflows. Upstream fences deep-interview continuations by owner/epoch, resumes incomplete rounds, suppresses stale goal reminders without suppressing fresh generations, holds repeated timeout continuations, and re-mints stale final-aggregate receipts so completion cannot deadlock.

JWC maps these surfaces to `jaw-interview`, `goal`, and the fused ultragoal runtime. The porting question is not naming alone: continuation ownership must survive user input and competing callbacks, while receipt recovery must remain compatible with JWC's fail-closed goal verification.

## Worktree Verification

JWC has dedicated `jaw-interview-runtime.ts`, `jaw-interview-round-validation.ts`, `goal-engine.ts`, and `goal-guard.ts` owners. Goal verification requires a fresh `final-aggregate` receipt, checks plan generation and ledger identity, and has extensive ultragoal runtime coverage. `agent-session.ts` also tracks reminder state and waits for background continuation settlement.

The focused sweep finds no explicit jaw-interview continuation-owner/epoch fence or incomplete-round resume contract matching the upstream deep-interview tests. It also finds no named timeout-continuation guard or reminder-generation contract. JWC's fresh-receipt validation is strong and partially equivalent, but the upstream stale final-aggregate re-mint recovery path is not visible in the guard/runtime search, so a stale receipt can still be a separate deadlock-risk question rather than a proven parity point.

## Closure — ADAPT, partial implementation with tracked residual

JWC already fences scheduled follow-up turns with prompt generations, deduplicates a goal reminder for the same assistant timestamp, and fail-closes goal completion when a `final-aggregate` receipt is missing, malformed, or stale. Those are meaningful local equivalents, but they do not prove upstream-style jaw-interview continuation-owner/epoch fencing, incomplete-round recovery, or stale receipt re-minting.
