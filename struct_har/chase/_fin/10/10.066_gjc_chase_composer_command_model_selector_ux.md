## WP5 Reconciliation — 2026-07-11

**Decision: ACCEPT.** JWC already carries the command/composer contracts needed by this card, and the remaining
model-selector deltas were adapted without replacing the curated TUI structure.

- `/model` is registered for both text/ACP and TUI use; bare TUI invocation opens `ModelSelectorComponent`.
- Composer keybinding and prompt-action discovery remain covered by the existing editor/autocomplete implementation.
- The selector now places programmatic search prefills at the end of the input, matching normal typing expectations.
- The ALL/preset landing now shows the effective session model and effort plus the current role assignments. It keeps
  JWC's grouped preset browser, provider tabs, role badges, and Enter-driven action menus.
- WP1's catalog, profile activation, and effort persistence remain the source of truth; WP5 only refreshes their
  effective values in the visible selector after model/profile changes.
