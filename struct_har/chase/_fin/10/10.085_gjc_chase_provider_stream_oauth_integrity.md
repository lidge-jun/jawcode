JWC owners are `packages/ai/src/providers/`, `packages/ai/src/stream.ts`, `packages/agent/src/agent.ts`, image-placeholder guards, and the coding-agent provider command/session glue. All six commits are G1 absorb candidates; Bedrock is an adapt/import split because local credential precedence may differ.

## Risk Notes

Do not normalize a provider's wire tool name back to an internal name after streaming begins. Start-block arguments and subsequent deltas must merge exactly once. Credential-chain tests must isolate environment variables and child processes to prevent developer credentials from masking failures. Placeholder validation should reject only truly orphaned references, not valid resumed-session blobs.
