## Chase Scope

This card joins two changes that share the model selector and composer lifecycle but require separate product decisions. The durable-selection slice promotes an RPC or SDK model choice into settings, session state, transcript state, and thinking level as one transaction; deferred or failed persistence must restore the previous selection. The pet slice adds an opt-in terminal companion, capability-aware skin selection, deterministic overlay teardown, listener cleanup, and composer restoration after ask dialogs.

JWC already has model-role persistence, temporary and durable model selection, RPC model commands, session rollback state, and thinking-level handling. No Gajae Pet source or test surface was found. Durable selection is therefore an adapt-and-verify candidate; the pet remains an explicit product adoption question and must not be smuggled in as a persistence fix.
