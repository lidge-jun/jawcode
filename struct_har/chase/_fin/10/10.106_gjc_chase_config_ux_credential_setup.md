## Chase Scope

This card tracks configuration as a user-facing integrity boundary. Doctor output must preserve structured values while redacting secrets. Provider onboarding and credential import must resolve the chosen source, store keys in the intended authority, retain custom credentials during confirmed overwrite, validate interactive values, and avoid echoing repeated init options. Keybinding migration and selector consolidation must not corrupt persisted preferences or collapse on narrow terminals.

JWC already validates and redacts provider setup, requires explicit overwrite confirmation, redacts secret-like config values, migrates keybindings, and exposes custom-provider and settings selectors. The focused sweep did not find upstream's config-doctor action, credential-auto-import module, SDK-embedding guide/tests, consolidated `chrome.ts`, or UX adversarial suite at matching paths. The chase therefore needs a contract-level comparison across JWC's existing config, onboarding, auth-storage, and selector owners.
