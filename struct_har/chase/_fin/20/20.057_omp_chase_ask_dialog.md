## Chase Scope

OMP turns ask into a multi-step dialog with multi-select gating, guest answers, row-specific notes, bounded titles and viewport, input-reset countdowns, and a distinct chat-redirect result. The behavioral contract matters more than the standalone `ask-dialog.ts` component.

The supplemented commits cover neighboring plan-review and todo intent edges: reflow ends, copy, scroll preservation, question-sensitive reminder suppression, terminal-user-message qualification, and explicit removal intent. These should remain a separate sub-seam so dialog adoption does not accidentally change JWC's todo lifecycle.

## Worktree Verification

JWC's `tools/ask.ts` already supports multiple questions, multi-select, previous-result navigation, prefill, timeout, and local/remote answer racing through `notifications/ask-bridge.ts`; Telegram has a dedicated inline-keyboard path. JWC has no standalone `ask-dialog.ts`, and the targeted sweep did not establish OMP's guest identity, input-reset countdown, row-note, or chat-redirect result. Plan-review/todo owners exist under JWC-specific paths and need separate behavioral checks.

| probe | result | JWC interpretation |
|---|---|---|

| Local/Telegram answer race | **exists and JWC-specific** | Preserve one result schema and exactly-one winner semantics. |
| Standalone rich ask dialog | **missing** | Component parity is optional; behavior parity is the target. |
| Plan-review/todo residuals | **owner surfaces exist** | Split from ask-dialog work and verify intent guards independently. |
