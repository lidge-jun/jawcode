This card tracks lifecycle failures where useful work exists but cleanup, cancellation, or a transport boundary loses it. The OMP delta covers browser acquisition and selector watchdogs, isolated-shell cancellation, agentic commit ordering, stderr/postmortem cleanup, internal URL recovery, launch PTY rendering, and in-process coreutils behavior.

JWC owns comparable browser, shell, commit, session, Markdown, and postmortem surfaces, but its implementation layout differs. The card therefore keeps the OMP changes as evidence for focused adaptation; it does not authorize a broad browser/runtime replacement or a vendored-coreutils import.
