## Chase Scope

This card covers the render boundary from a live assistant/tool frame to canonical terminal scrollback. The portable contract is not OMP's component layout; it is stable table and diff coordinates, preservation of meaningful blank rows, exactly-once commitment of completed rows, disposal of stale deferred output at session boundaries, and non-destructive repaint after resize.

JWC has a stronger, independently evolved viewport/scrollback implementation in `packages/tui/src/tui.ts`, plus live assistant ownership in `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/controllers/event-controller.ts`. OMP's `transcript-container.ts` and `chat-transcript-builder.ts` therefore remain architectural references. Terminal input fallback, cmux notification routing, Kitty/IME anchoring, loader scheduling, and bounded preview tails are separable boundary checks rather than one porting bundle.

All 46 commits recorded by D08 are retained below. Changelog-only effects are evidence, not standalone implementation work.
