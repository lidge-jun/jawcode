# 20.059 — omp chase: advisor steering and terminal blocker safety

> MOC: [20_omp_chase_MOC](./20_omp_chase_MOC.md) · G2 · ⬜ · P2 — preserve late blockers while quarantining untrusted advisor payloads.
> Reviewed source: OMP `7aa1d581c..b0d04e517` (`origin/main`, v16.4.2→v17.0.1) vs JWC worktree.

## Chase Scope

This card tracks the ordering and trust boundary around asynchronous review. OMP preserves a real blocker that arrives after a terminal answer, opens its steering immune window only after a turn is actually steered, and carries terminal notes across ACP deferral. At the same time it quarantines unknown tool responses and native payloads, accepts only provenance-linked advisor results, and annotates stale/coalesced deltas.

JWC has no OMP-equivalent `packages/coding-agent/src/advisor/` runtime. Its comparable safety surfaces are task receipts, workflow-state receipts, goal/workflow gates, and `agent-session.ts`. Direct file translation is therefore rejected. The chase target is a JWC-native invariant: a late verified blocker must remain actionable, while uncorrelated review payloads must never become executable steering.

All 21 D09 commits are retained. Documentation and test-only commits remain anchors for the intended contract.
