## Chase Scope

This card covers four tightly coupled OMP seams. Cursor tool calls gain live-model resolution, mounted-device exposure, approval gating, advisor-aware delete handling, protocol-end completion, and duplicate-MCP suppression. The xdev follow-up centralizes transport lifecycle, preserves presentation and capabilities across tool rebuilds, gates startup mounts, and recognizes checkpoint/rewind results. Advisor fixes add fallback chains, terminal-failure cutoffs, transcript reconciliation, and usage-anchored context maintenance. Plan fixes preserve role-model restoration and close or defer the review overlay at safe asynchronous boundaries.

JWC already has a Cursor provider, coding-agent cursor handlers, checkpoint/rewind state, plan-model switching, and a plan-review UI. It does not have OMP's `advisor/runtime.ts`, xdev naming, or standalone `plan-mode/model-transition.ts`. The chase target is therefore behavioral ownership: one live tool-resolution path, one approval boundary for mounted execution, reversible plan-model transitions, and no second advisor orchestration subsystem by accident.

All 32 commits whose messages or source ownership match Cursor, advisor, xdev/mounted-device, plan-role/model, or plan-review-overlay behavior are retained. The two plan-review commits are classified here rather than in the general TUI card because their correctness depends on execution dispatch and plan state.

- [ ] Preserve exactly-once Cursor tool and MCP execution.
- [ ] Require approval before mounted device execution or native deletion.
- [ ] Keep xdev transport, presentation, and capability state transactional across rebuilds.
- [ ] Preserve checkpoint/rewind semantics independently of xdev implementation details.
- [ ] Restore plan role/model and overlay state only after the owning async transition settles.
- [ ] Reject a direct OMP advisor-runtime port unless JWC approves that product surface.
