## Chase Scope

This card separates two error authorities that must never be conflated. Anthropic, Google Gemini CLI/shared, and OpenAI Completions can report policy or safety stops through different wire fields; JWC needs one stable session/ACP meaning while retaining the original provider evidence. A managed fallback can also fail locally before or between provider attempts; that failure must remain a client/transport fact rather than being relabeled as a provider refusal.

JWC already maps several safety indicators in provider adapters and recognizes refusal-like messages in `agent-session.ts`. It does not have the same-path upstream `fallback-transport.ts` or `provider-safety-stop.ts` owners. The chase therefore begins with an inventory of existing normalization and retry decisions, then chooses one canonical typed boundary rather than layering another string-only classifier.
