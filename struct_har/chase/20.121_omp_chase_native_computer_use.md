**Classification: split · autonomy bucket: C.** OMP adds a Rust desktop addon, direct screenshot/input execution, `/computer`, Codex computer-call wire handling, and platform build dependencies. JWC already exposes `computer_use`, but through a lazy driver backend; native capture/input and its approval/platform policy are a separate product decision.
